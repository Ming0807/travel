# Research Evaluation Layer Design Decision

Date: 2026-08-08

Status: Proposed for advisor approval

Canonical blueprint: `docs/research/RESEARCH_BLUEPRINT.md`

## Decision

Use a focused Design Science Research pilot with mixed-method evaluation.

The project will evaluate five connected outcomes:

1. the Smart Tourism artifact;
2. observed behavior in the real system flow;
3. tourism data generated by real field participants;
4. dashboard-supported decisions by tourism operators.
5. feedback-supported improvements by attraction/site managers.

The research layer must be separate from operational data collection. It will add versioned studies, instruments, research sessions, research-specific consent, evaluation responses, operator tasks, and de-identified exports without turning every production visit into a research record.

## Selected Scope

- Primary geography: controlled Yala pilot sites.
- Tourist/system-user sample planning range: 60-100 completed participants, subject to advisor-approved sampling and power analysis.
- Operator stakeholder group: 10-15 task-based sessions and interviews.
- Analysis: descriptive statistics, scale reliability, session-level funnels, exploratory associations, operator task evidence, and thematic coding.
- Explicitly excluded: broad population claims, automatic mixing of simulated and real data, and unsupported SEM/causal claims.

## Key Architecture Decisions

- Research invitation and consent occur before research-specific tracking; decline continues the normal product flow.
- `field_observation`, `simulated_usability`, and `pilot_internal` are controlled collection modes.
- Research session is the correlation unit; funnel event is not a participant.
- Instrument wording and consent are versioned and frozen before final collection.
- The pilot measures cumulative journey burden; item reduction happens before freeze when predefined time, completion, missingness, or comprehension triggers fail.
- Research analytics live under a separate `/admin/research` workspace.
- Attraction recommendations expose their evidence and connect reviewed issues to owned improvement actions and follow-up periods.
- Research export is study-scoped, de-identified, and excludes direct identities, raw photos, and private storage paths.
- The current `checkin_codes.campaign_id` placeholder will not be used without a real relational contract.
- `attraction_feedback_issues` and `attraction_improvement_actions` are permanent production functions, while stakeholder task answers remain research data.
- Runtime AI is not required. The pilot uses transparent SQL/statistical rules and human-reviewed issue categories; paid/external AI is a future optional experiment, not current scope.

## Preconditions

Implementation begins only after advisor approval of the research title, RQs, instruments, sampling/power approach, ethics/consent procedure, and analysis plan.

The implementation backlog is maintained in `tasks/PHASE_18_RESEARCH_EVALUATION_LAYER.md`.
